FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to common questions about SchemeGuard and how it works.

Who can submit an AFS to SchemeGuard?

Submissions must be made by an authorised party — typically a managing agent, bank, conveyancer or body corporate trustee. Individual owners cannot submit on behalf of a body corporate.

How is the composite score calculated?

The composite score combines a Financial Health score (60% weight) and an MRRP Reserve Fund score (40% weight), each rated on a 0–5 scale. Scores above 4.5 are Adequate; below 1.5 are Critical.

How do I get access to the SchemeGuard platform?

Access is granted via invitation. Contact us to request access. Users are authenticated via Microsoft Entra ID and must be registered in the system before logging in.

How long does it take to receive a report?

Once AFS data has been submitted and validated, the SchemeGuard report is generated immediately. Pre-purchase reports are typically delivered within 48 hours of AFS receipt.

Is my scheme's data kept confidential?

Yes. Data is only shared with organisations that have a signed Sharing Agreement in place. Scheme names are anonymised in any benchmarking or pilot analysis presented to third parties.

What is the MRRP and why does it matter?

The Maintenance, Repair and Replacement Plan is a statutory requirement under PMR 22 of the STSMA Regulations. It requires a 10-year capital plan for all common property components. Most schemes do not comply.

Can I request a correction to my scheme's data?

Yes. Contact support@zatisvy.com with the scheme name, submission year and the correction required. We will review and update within 5 business days.

What does the RAG rating mean?

Green = Adequate or Low Risk (composite ≥ 3.5). Amber = Moderate (2.5–3.5). Red = High Risk or Critical (below 2.5). The RAG rating provides an at-a-glance summary for banks and trustees.

User Roles

What Each Role Can Do

SchemeGuard has role-based access controls. Your access level depends on the role assigned by your organisation.

🔑 Admin

✓Full access to all screens and data
✓User management and sharing agreements
✓AFS data capture and submission
✓Power BI reports and paginated reports

📝 Capturer

✓AFS data capture and submission
✓View schemes assigned to their organisation
✗No access to admin screens or user management
✗No Power BI access

📊 Analyst

✓Power BI reports and dashboards
✓Paginated scheme health reports
✗No data capture access
✗No admin screens

⚡ Power User

✓AFS data capture and submission
✓Power BI reports and dashboards
✗No admin screens or user management
